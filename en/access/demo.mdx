description: "A one-time paid trial for one wallet."

**Demo costs 1 USDC and provides 7 days of access from purchase.** An address can buy Demo only once. It is a paid trial pass with no renewal or automatic recurring charges.

## Main rules

- Duration is exactly 7 days measured by block time.
- Time runs even when you are not using the app.
- The pass stays in your wallet after expiry but stops granting access.
- Transfers, sales, renewal, early cancellation, and recovery to another address are not supported.
- Buying Demo does not commit you to Standard or Gold.

The limit applies to an address, not a unique person. Another wallet has its own eligibility but does not inherit your previous cohorts or settings.

## Moving to another pass

You can buy Standard or Gold at any time. Standard starts its own 30 days immediately. Unused Demo days do not extend that period.

For example, buying Standard on day five of Demo creates a separate 30-day period. To use all of Demo before Standard begins, wait until Demo expires.

## Sale changes

An administrator can change Demo pricing without a seven-day delay, pause purchases, or close the sale. An existing Demo continues counting down. The contract has no administrative reset of used Demo eligibility or revocation of an active Demo period.

See the [contract directory](/en/contracts/addresses) for the address, sale state, and verification date. Shared payment rules are on the [pricing page](/en/access/pricing).
