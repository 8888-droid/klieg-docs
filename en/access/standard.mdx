title: "Standard — 30 days"
description: "Monthly access with renewal during the final seven days."
---

**Standard V2 costs 5 USDC for 30 days.** Each purchase pays for one period. New Standard purchases use the V2 contract.

## First purchase

Access starts immediately and lasts exactly 30 days. Your wallet receives a non-transferable NFT. Access duration is determined by the contract record, not merely the presence of the NFT or its wallet image.

## Renewal

Renewal opens when **no more than 7 days remain**, and is also available after expiry.

- If Standard V2 is still active, 30 days are added to its existing expiry.
- If it has expired, a new period starts at purchase.
- Renewal is unavailable while more than 7 days remain.
- You cannot buy several periods in one transaction or accumulate unlimited future time. The maximum horizon after renewal is 37 days.

**Example:** renewing with 4 days left gives 34 days until expiry. Buying 2 days after expiry gives 30 days from the new purchase; the gap is not recovered.

## Continuity

The pass keeps the same token ID across later purchases. **ACCESS SINCE** refers to continuous Standard V2 access and restarts after a gap. It does not import Demo or Standard V1 time.

## Cancellation, revocation, and pause

The contract lets a holder end their subscription early, without an onchain refund. An authorized administrator can also shorten an active period through revocation. The NFT remains, and a later eligible purchase can start a new period.

Pausing or closing sales blocks purchases and renewals but does not stop the clock on paid access. Holder cancellation is unavailable while paused; administrative revocation remains possible.

## Legacy Standard V1

V1 sales and renewals closed on September 5, 2026. Existing paid time is preserved within its recorded expiry. V2 is a separate contract; a new V2 purchase does not carry over unused V1 days.

Check the [current and legacy addresses](/en/contracts/addresses). There are no automatic recurring payments.
