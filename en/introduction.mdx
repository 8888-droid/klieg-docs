title: "What is Klieg Wire?"
description: "A personal radar for callers, theses, and wallet activity."
---

**Klieg Wire helps you discover tokens through the people you follow and check whether their actions support their words.** Choose callers, read their theses, and inspect available evidence of purchases, holdings, and exits.

The product focuses on three questions:

- Who brought attention to a token, and what did they actually say?
- Which wallet is linked to the caller, and what is confirmed onchain?
- What happened after the call: did the caller hold, reduce, or exit?

Klieg brings posts, profiles, tokens, and wallet actions into one workspace. Each conclusion preserves the distinction between a caller's statement, a platform observation, and independent onchain confirmation.

## Who it is for

Crypto market participants who care about a caller's history, the context of a specific thesis, and verifiable facts. Researchers who connect profiles and wallets. Users who want to build their own circle of callers and see where their interests converge.

## Product status

These docs describe the beta as of **September 7, 2026**. See [coverage](/en/concepts/coverage) for feature and network availability, and the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for development priorities.

Klieg provides information for independent research. The current version does not buy or sell the tokens you research, copy trades, or manage your funds. A high signal score or a caller's successful history does not guarantee an outcome.
