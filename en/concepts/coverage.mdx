**Product-status snapshot: September 7, 2026.** This describes beta coverage, not real-time service availability.

## Publication platforms

| Platform | Role in Klieg | Current coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pump | Caller profiles, theses, and related observations | Available in the beta |
| Fomo | Profiles, theses, and activity alongside Pump | Available in the beta; continuity of fresh data remains under evaluation |
| X | Profile references and evidence linking accounts | Does not imply ingestion of all X posts |
| Telegram | Researched profiles and relationship evidence | An automated Telegram feed is not currently claimed |

Publication platform and token network are separate attributes. A post on one platform can discuss a token on another network.

## Networks

| Network | Current role |
| --- | --- |
| Robinhood | Token data and available independent EVM-event confirmations |
| Base | Token data, available independent confirmations, and all Klieg access contracts |
| Solana | Solana tokens may appear in posts; independent Solana confirmation is disabled in the current deployment |

A Solana card does not imply that its position was independently checked onchain. EVM-network support also does not imply coverage of every token, wallet, venue, or historical event.

## Working with coverage limits

Check the latest observation time and evidence labels. If fresh events temporarily stop arriving, older cards remain history rather than confirmation of the current situation.

Interpret empty results carefully: they can reflect your cohort, filters, missing observations, or limited coverage. See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for development priorities.
