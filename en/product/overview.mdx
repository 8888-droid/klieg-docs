The workspace takes you from a broad overview to an individual, verifiable event. The section names below match the app's English interface labels.

| Section | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Explore tokens and activity in Global or your caller cohort |
| Signals | Inspect individual theses and signals, their relevance, and evidence |
| Callers | Find a caller, open linked profiles, and assess their call history |
| Token | Compare callers, theses, market context, and wallet actions for one token |
| Watchlist | Return to specific saved signals |
| Research | Submit evidence connecting profiles |
| Profile | Check your login wallet, passes, and access expiry |

## Global and My cohort

**Global** helps you discover callers and ideas outside your current circle. **My cohort** scopes the overview to a selected group. A token may appear in both views, but the callers and cohort-related events can differ.

When using filters, check the selected network, activity type, cohort, and time range. An empty result may mean the loaded data contains no matching events.

## A useful workflow

1. Choose Global or your cohort.
2. Check why a token appeared: a call, a confirmed purchase, or a combination.
3. Open the thesis and caller profile.
4. Review timestamps, sources, and available onchain evidence.
5. Save the signal to your Watchlist if you want to revisit its history.

## Updates

Incoming events can change signal order, counts, and assessments. In Signals, you can pause row reordering to inspect the list. When you return to the tab, the app catches up on available missed events; this does not imply every source remained available while you were away.
