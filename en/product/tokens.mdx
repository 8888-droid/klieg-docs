A token page groups information by **network + contract address**. Names and tickers help people read the page, but do not identify an asset: different tokens can share the same ticker.

## What to inspect

- Which callers discussed the token, when, and with what theses.
- How many distinct callers have confirmed purchases, holdings, or sales.
- Which market-cap, liquidity, and activity observations are available, including their dates and labels.
- Which linked-wallet actions are confirmed and how they relate to the calls.
- Which token facts are confirmed, unknown, or need clarification.

## From a count to a fact

Several posts do not necessarily represent several independent callers. A confirmed purchase does not mean the position is still held. Open individual theses and evidence, especially when significant time has passed since the original call.

## Market context

Market cap, volume, and liquidity help compare tokens. They are dated observations. They are not an executable quote for your trade and do not guarantee that a particular amount can be bought or sold at the displayed price.

## Token Truth

Token Truth aims to provide a fuller account of token origin, control, ownership, and exit options. The current beta includes selected checks and market context; complete coverage of routes and contract powers is still developing.

Unknown and unverified results remain distinct states. The absence of a warning should not be treated as a completed token assessment. See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap).
