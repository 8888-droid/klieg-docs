A **cohort** is a group of callers you select. It answers: which tokens are the people I follow calling or buying now?

## Working with a cohort

1. Find the profiles you want in Callers.
2. Create a cohort with a useful name.
3. Add callers and inspect the platform accounts included in each member.
4. Select the cohort in the overview and set your activity filters.

You can rename cohorts and add or remove members. Cohort membership belongs to your login wallet and persists across sessions. Signing in with another wallet does not open the original wallet's cohorts.

## One caller across platforms

When an account relationship is approved, Klieg can present the accounts as one caller with several source profiles. Each post retains its platform. Matching handles or avatars alone do not establish that relationship.

Following the approved identity as a whole is the natural default. Where account-only scope is available, it lets you limit observation to one platform.

## What convergence means

If several cohort members discuss the same token, open their individual theses and timestamps. Several posts by one person and several independent callers are different situations. Convergence helps prioritize research, but does not prove that callers are independent or that the token will perform well.

## Cohorts and Watchlist

A cohort follows **callers** and helps discover new tokens. Watchlist saves **specific signals** that already caught your attention. You can use both together.

Activity notifications are available inside the app. Creating a cohort does not connect Telegram alerts or execute trades.
