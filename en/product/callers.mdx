A caller profile brings together available posts, approved linked accounts, and wallet evidence. Account and wallet associations should have a basis you can investigate.

## How to research a caller

1. Check the original platform profiles and evidence behind their grouping.
2. Open several theses, including unsuccessful or stale ones.
3. Compare outcomes at the same horizon and check the sample size.
4. Inspect wallet activity before and after the call.

A single striking success does not describe the whole history. Missing observations and incomplete coverage also affect the sample.

## Fixed-horizon outcomes

Where suitable observations exist, Klieg measures outcomes **5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours, and 24 hours** after publication. Compare the same horizon. A missing measurement is not a zero return or evidence of failure.

Platform-derived market estimates retain their labels. They are not the caller's realized profit or the result of a trade available to a user.

## Caller accountability

Timing distinguishes holding or buying before the call, buying after it, reducing, and exiting. Several wallets selling around the same time does not by itself establish coordination, common ownership, or an intent to influence an audience.

## Relationships can change

Profile grouping follows reviewed evidence and can be reconsidered. Original posts retain their source. Use [Research](/en/product/research) to investigate a questionable relationship.
