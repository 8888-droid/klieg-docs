---
title: "Community research"
description: "Propose profile relationships and support them with verifiable sources."
---

In **Research**, users with the relevant access can submit evidence connecting two profiles. A submission enters review; it does not merge accounts or attribute a wallet to a caller by itself.

## Submit a contribution

1. Select two source profiles.
2. Choose the evidence type: a social profile, a wallet reference, or a cross-reference between accounts.
3. Add a source link and explain exactly what it supports.
4. For wallet evidence, specify the network and address.
5. Submit and follow the review outcome in your contribution history.

A strong submission explains a verifiable relationship. Similar names, matching pictures, or an unsupported assumption do not prove a common identity.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Awaiting review |
| Approved | Evidence accepted |
| Rejected | Evidence rejected |
| Superseded | Replaced by a later decision or submission |
| Revoked | A previously accepted decision was withdrawn |

Decisions can be reconsidered as new information appears. Changing a profile relationship does not rewrite the authorship of original posts.

## Reputation

A contributor's score reflects review outcomes and can change when decisions are reconsidered. Scout, Proven, and Trusted describe participation history. They do not grant permission to merge accounts independently.

Research contributions do not create a right to payment, equity, tokens, or an airdrop. Guided assignments and additional research tools are part of the [roadmap](/en/roadmap).
