## September 7, 2026 — RU / EN documentation

Two versions of the product documentation have been prepared with matching topics: the idea, workspace, evidence, pricing, passes, and contracts.

Prices and sale modes were checked at Base block **50,999,814**. This snapshot does not establish availability of every app feature or the current state of every publication platform.

## September 5, 2026 — Demo and Standard V2

- Demo deployed: 7 days, one purchase per address, initial price 1 USDC.
- Standard V2 deployed: 30 days per purchase, renewal in the final 7 days, initial price 5 USDC.
- Standard V1 sales and renewals closed while preserving previously paid time.
- The new access model distinguishes timed passes and preserves non-expiring Gold access.

## September 1, 2026 — Gold

Gold deployed as a non-transferable, non-expiring supporter pass with an initial price of 1,000 USDC per unit. Later units accrue on the same NFT. The contract includes a separate delayed recovery procedure.

Public addresses and transactions are in the [contract directory](/en/contracts/addresses). See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for future directions.
