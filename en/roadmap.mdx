**As of September 7, 2026.** These are development directions without fixed delivery-date commitments. A feature being available in beta does not mean all quality and coverage checks are complete.

## Available in the beta

- Theses and signals with social observations separated from onchain evidence.
- Caller cohorts and personalized token discovery.
- Approved grouping of a caller's accounts across platforms.
- Calls compared with confirmed purchases, reductions, and exits where evidence exists.
- Caller profiles, fixed-horizon outcomes, and signal dossiers.
- Community relationship research and reputation based on review outcomes.
- Access through Demo, Standard V2, and Gold on Base.

## Next focus: Token Truth

More complete token research should connect origin, control powers, ownership classification, and potential exit routes. The next stage also clarifies what was known at the time of a call and where sources disagree.

This extends the existing token context. The beta does not yet provide an exhaustive assessment of all tokens and trading routes.

## Quality of personal research

Work continues on checking cohorts across different users, account-grouping accuracy, understandable scoring reasons, and outcome interpretation. A separate goal is consistent arrival of fresh posts and clear communication of incomplete coverage.

## Guided community research

Planned work includes research assignments, prioritization, and an assignment lifecycle. Existing relationship contributions and evidence review provide the foundation. This direction does not promise monetary rewards or tokens.

## Broader confirmation coverage

Independent Solana verification is a future extension after the necessary quality checks. Network and platform availability is documented under [coverage](/en/concepts/coverage).

Trading and automated copy trading are outside this product roadmap. Klieg's development focuses on discovering ideas and verifying callers' actions.
